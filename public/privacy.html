Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

What we store

Only what a game needs: the game's name, reveal day, mission choice, budget and message; each player's name (or nickname) and the favourite things they choose to fill in; who looks after whom; the notes players send each other inside the game; each player's guess; and whether a note was reported.

We do not ask for email addresses, phone numbers or passwords. Your place in a game is a random key saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We store only a one-way hash of it.

Who can see what

Everyone with the game link sees the game details and the list of names. Only you see who you look after and your notes. Your mortal sees your notes as coming from "Your angel". If you report a note, the organiser sees that note with the sender's name. After the organiser reveals the game, everyone with the link sees who looked after whom, the guesses and how many notes each angel sent. The text of private notes is never shown to anyone else.

How long we keep it

A game and everything in it is deleted automatically 14 days after its reveal, or 120 days after it was created if it is never revealed. The organiser can delete a game at any time.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics that identify you, no selling or sharing of data. To prevent abuse we briefly keep a counter per IP address (rate limiting), which is deleted within an hour.

Age

Featherpost is meant for players aged 13 and over. Groups of younger children should be run by a teacher or parent.

Where it runs

The service runs on Cloudflare (hosting and database).

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on the support page.

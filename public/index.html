Questions

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. Your mortal sees your notes as coming from "Your angel". Nobody, including the organiser, sees who has whom until reveal day. The one exception: if a note is reported, the organiser can see who sent it.

Do people need an account or the app?

No. The link opens in any browser, including inside WhatsApp or Messenger. Your place in the game is saved on your phone, and you can copy a personal link to open it on another device.

How is this different from Secret Santa?

Secret Santa is one gift on one day. A secret angel game runs for days or weeks: anonymous notes back and forth, a mission or gift theme each day or week, guessing, and a reveal at the end.

How many people can play?

From 3 to 80 people per game.

What if someone sends something unkind?

Obvious insults are blocked. Any note can be reported with one tap: it disappears for you and the organiser sees it with the sender's name.

What happens to our data?

We keep only the names, favourites and notes the game needs, and delete everything 14 days after the reveal. No tracking, no ads. See the privacy policy.
